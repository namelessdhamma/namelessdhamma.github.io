Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026

Nameless Dhamma is an educational Dhamma project. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Nameless Dhamma services that connect with TikTok.

Information we may receive

When you authorize the TikTok integration, TikTok may provide basic account information permitted by the scopes you approve, such as an open identifier, display name, and avatar. The integration may also receive authorization tokens needed to upload content to your TikTok account as drafts when you explicitly authorize that capability.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide, operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the requested Nameless Dhamma integration, including TikTok login and authorized content uploading. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and retention

We retain only the information reasonably necessary to operate the integration, meet security and legal obligations, and diagnose failures. Authorization credentials and tokens are treated as sensitive data and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Sharing

We do not share personal information with advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the integration, subject to their own security and privacy obligations, or disclosed when required by law.

Your choices

You can revoke TikTok authorization using TikTok's account or app settings. You may also contact us to request deletion of data associated with the integration where applicable.

Third-party services

TikTok and other third-party platforms are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Nameless Dhamma is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.
